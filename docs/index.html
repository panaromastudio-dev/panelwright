Six systems that usually cost six programs

Workflow

From the model to a priced job in under two minutes

1
Model

Build cabinets with the generator, drop in a template, or draw your own. Panelwright reads thickness, size, material and exposed edges from the geometry itself.

2
Calculate

One button: cut list, nesting per material, hardware take-off, joinery connectors, drilling and full costing — using your databases and shop standard.

3
Check

The shop-rule check flags what would fail on the saw or at assembly: parts larger than the sheet, non-standard slide lengths, doors too wide for their hinges, missing materials.

4
Deliver

Quote and invoice for the client. DXF, drilling, labels and the shop packet for the floor. Stock deducted, purchase order raised, job archived, profit tracked.

Nesting of a 3.6 m kitchen: 48 parts, 6 sheets, 91% utilisation on sheet 1. Offcuts above the minimum size go straight to the store.

Cabinet generator

A kitchen you can actually build

Not decorative boxes. Every rule in the generator came from a job that went wrong before the rule existed — plinth recessed for toes, doors that do not foul the wall unit, drawer depths that match a slide you can buy.

28 types, one dialog

Base, drawers, sink, hob, dishwasher, wall, lift-up, tall, oven / fridge / microwave towers, blind and 45° corners, island, wardrobe with inner drawers, pull-out larder, filler, end panel, cornice, round end.

Corners done right

The 45° corner has both return sides, a pentagon worktop, a three-piece plinth and a diagonal door outside the carcass — a closed box, like a real one.

Runs from wall lengths

Straight, L and U layouts: plinth, worktop with sink and hob cut-outs, splashback and hood included. Wall units sit above the base run automatically.

Two Ø1260 table tops — the corners of each blank take 170×170 shelves. Corner parts are marked, and a warning tells a panel-saw shop to switch it off.

CNC and beam saw

Round parts are round

Most cut-list tools see a bounding box. Panelwright keeps the real contour: the nesting view draws it, the DXF exports it on its own layer with the blank on BLANK, edge banding is counted by circumference, and small parts are nested into the corners of the blank when the shop cuts on a router.

System 32 drilling comes out as a drawing per part — 35 mm cups, 45/9.5 plate pattern, Ø5 shelf pins on a 32 mm pitch, and confirmat, Minifix or dowel holes according to the joinery you chose.

FAQ

Questions shops ask before they buy

Is this really a whole system, or a plugin with extras?

It is a system in the sense that matters to a small shop: one set of databases feeds design, production, purchasing, paperwork and profit, and each job updates stock, offcuts and the archive for the next one. It is not a factory MRP — no machine scheduling, no accounting integration, no multi-site server. For a one- to twenty-person shop it replaces the plugin, the spreadsheets and the Word templates.

Does it work with my own drawn cabinets, not only the generator?

Yes. Any group or component with a panel-like shape becomes a part; nested groups become cabinets. The generator is a shortcut, not a requirement.

Which SketchUp versions?

SketchUp 2021 and newer, Pro or Studio, on Windows and macOS. Free web SketchUp cannot run extensions.

Can two or three designers use it together?

Yes, through one shared folder on a network drive or a synced folder. Prices, archive, stock and offcuts are shared; file locking and atomic writes stop one person's save from wiping another's. Each seat needs its own key.

What happens to my data if I stop paying, or change computers?

Everything is in your own folders in plain JSON and normal files — nothing is locked in a cloud. One backup file moves prices, hardware, archive and offcuts to a new machine. A perpetual licence keeps working forever.

Is the nesting good enough for CNC?

For rectangular parts it is a MaxRects nesting with kerf, margins and offcut re-use, and shaped parts keep their true contour in the DXF with small parts filling the blank corners. Full free-form nesting is best left to your CAM software — Panelwright gives it the contours it needs.

Can I use my own hardware, not the catalogue brands?

Yes. Add your own hardware with its dimensions, price and drilling, and even point it at your own .skp model so it appears correctly in 3D.

What does the trial include?

Everything, for 10 days, on one computer, without a credit card. When it ends your projects, prices and settings stay — a key just unlocks them again.
