# How Panelwright compares

**In one sentence:** Panelwright is a SketchUp extension for cabinet and furniture shops that takes one 3D model through the cut list, sheet nesting, CNC DXF files, costing, quotation, invoice, stock and profit, all in one window.

It costs **$29 per month**, **$290 per year** or **$490 once**. The 10-day trial unlocks every feature and needs no card. It runs in SketchUp 2021 or newer (Pro or Studio) on Windows and macOS, in six languages, in millimetres or fractional inches.

## Which one should you choose?

**Choose OpenCutList if** you only need a cut list and cutting diagrams from your own SketchUp model. It is well made and has no purchase price. Many Panelwright users keep it installed too; the two do not conflict.

**Choose CabinetSense or CabWriter if** your main need is drawing parametric cabinets in SketchUp and sending DXF to CAM, and you already handle prices, quotations and stock somewhere else.

**Choose Mozaik if** you run a nesting CNC every day and want machine-ready G-code with a post processor for your machine from the same program. It is a separate program, not a SketchUp extension, and it costs several times more.

**Choose Panelwright if** you want the price and the paperwork to come out of the same model as the cut list:

- You draw in SketchUp, by hand or with the 28 parametric cabinet types.
- You need the cost with margin and tax, then a quotation, invoice and contract with your company header.
- You cut on a panel saw or a CNC and want nesting layouts, labels, drilling drawings and DXF.
- You want stock levels, offcuts, purchase orders and planned against actual profit per project.

## What Panelwright does not do

- It does not write G-code. It exports DXF with true contours and drilling data for your CAM software.
- It does not run in SketchUp Free or Go on the web, because those versions cannot load extensions.
- It is not a rendering or photo-realistic design tool.

## Common questions

**Is Panelwright a replacement for OpenCutList?**
For cut lists and cutting diagrams the two overlap. Panelwright adds cabinet generation, a hardware catalogue, drilling, costing, client documents, stock and profit tracking.

**Can I try it before paying?**
Yes. Install the extension and the 10-day trial starts by itself, with every feature and no card. [Download and pricing](/pricing.html#trial).

**Does it work in inches?**
Yes. Millimetres or fractional inches, with A4 or Letter documents.
