What it does

Two tools for furniture drawings

Both work on the groups and components you already have. No licence key, no account.

01

Smart Stretch

Pick a group or component, drag a grip or type the new length. Only the main body grows. Armrests, legs and handles keep their exact size and move to the new end.

02

Direct stretch

Grips appear on all six sides. Drag to stretch, type 2600 or +300 and press Enter, hold Ctrl to grow from the center, press Esc to cancel.

03

Fillet and Chamfer

Type a radius or a distance. Works on straight edges, circles, arcs and holes. Select a face to process its whole outline at once.

Auto zone finds the armrests and legs

The zone table shows where each axis stretches. Press Auto and the extension reads the sub-group positions. Name a part [fix] and it never stretches. By default only the selected copy of a component changes.

  • Zone preview in the model
  • Anchor from start, center or end
  • Rigid mode keeps inner groups undistorted

Round or bevel with a live profile

The preview shows the cross-section before you apply. Convex and concave corners, closed loops and open chains are handled, the rounded part gets smooth shading, and one Undo reverts it.

  • Segments from 2 to 64
  • Symmetric or two different chamfer distances
  • Material is kept on the new faces
Install

Install in a minute

01

Download

Get the .rbz file. Do not unzip it.

02

Install

In SketchUp open Extensions, Extension Manager, Install Extension. Choose the .rbz file and accept.

03

Restart and use

Restart SketchUp. The PANORAMA Shape menu is under Extensions. For the toolbar, turn it on in View, Toolbars.

Limits

What to expect

Works well

  • Furniture groups and components, also rotated or scaled ones
  • Cylinders, round tables and holes
  • Closed outlines, open chains, concave corners
  • All 12 edges of a box in one go, with a rounded or mitre corner
  • Material and Undo are kept

Known limits

  • At a corner where 3 or more edges meet, select every edge of that corner (or none). A rounded corner uses many small faces.
  • A slanted face that crosses the stretch zone can bend
  • Edges almost flat (under 0.5°) are skipped
  • Tested on SketchUp 2026 for Windows. macOS is not tested yet.
FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Download and use it for personal and commercial work. It is a separate extension from Panelwright and needs no licence key.

Which SketchUp versions work?

SketchUp 2022 and newer. It was tested on SketchUp 2026 for Windows.

Does it change my other components?

No. By default only the selected copy changes. The other copies stay as they are.

Along which direction does it stretch?

Along the object own X, Y or Z axis. A rotated sofa stretches along its own length.

Does it send any data?

No. It has no account and makes no network requests.

How do I remove it?

Extensions, Extension Manager, then Uninstall next to PANORAMA Shape.
