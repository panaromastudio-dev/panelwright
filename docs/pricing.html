How buying works

Checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy (card, PayPal, Apple Pay; VAT invoices for EU businesses). You receive the download and an order key straight away. Install Panelwright, open Extensions → Panelwright → Licence to see your Computer ID, then get your licence key at panelwright.app/activate — it takes about a second, at any hour. The key is tied to that computer; moving to another one is a click on the same page.

Businesses in Mongolia: local invoicing in ₮ is available — write to support@panelwright.app.

FAQ

Pricing questions

Which plan should I pick?

Try free for 10 days first. If Panelwright is part of your weekly routine, annual is the cheapest per month; if you dislike subscriptions, perpetual with 12 months of updates is the usual choice for a one-person shop.

What does "perpetual" mean exactly?

The version you have on the day your 12 months of updates end keeps working forever, with its key, on your computer. Updates after that are optional at $145 per year — you can also skip years and re-join later.

Can I move the licence to another computer?

Yes. Deactivate on the old machine from the Licence dialog (or write to support if the machine is gone) and activate on the new one. Team seats work the same way.

Windows and Mac?

One key works on either. SketchUp 2021 or newer, Pro or Studio.

Do students get a discount?

Yes — 50% on annual for students and teachers with a school email. Write to support.

How do I cancel?

From the customer portal link in your receipt, in one click. Access continues to the end of the paid period.

Refunds?

14 days, no questions, for any plan. Send the order number to support@panelwright.app.

Is the Extension Warehouse price the same?

Yes. The Warehouse sells the annual and perpetual plans through Trimble; buying here adds the monthly option and gives you the key immediately.
